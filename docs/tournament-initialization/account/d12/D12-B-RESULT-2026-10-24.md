## Boundary matrix

- Tournament policy: `T+89 days = RETAIN`; `T+90 days = ELIGIBLE_FOR_REVIEW`; `T+91 days = ELIGIBLE_FOR_REVIEW`, for both `FINALIZED` and `CANCELLED` fixtures.
- Actual tournament cleanup: terminal `Tournament`, enrollment and ACK rows remained after the real retention service ran. Operational invitation/import rows were cleaned separately. This distinction is the D12-F03 finding.
- Cross-scope cleanup: running cleanup for Tournament A left Tournament B enrollment, staff assignment, invitation, ACK, import ledger and audit counts unchanged.
- Idempotency: first eligible run changed the operational records; replay at the same time changed zero additional records.
- Recovery: before-expiry token was accepted; at-expiry and after-expiry tokens were rejected. Expired/consumed secret material was cleared at the exact boundary and the account lifecycle was unchanged.
- Staff invitation: expired pending invitations became `EXPIRED`; an accepted `ACTIVE` assignment remained active.
- Audit: 364-, 365- and 366-day records all remained because no 365-day policy/enforcement exists. The test proves the gap; it does not delete audit rows.
- Audit append-only: a D08 acknowledgement operation appended a later event; the original event content remained unchanged. No normal audit update/delete path was observed.
- Sanitization handoff: retention-generated audit records did not copy the synthetic token-like `reason`; the sentinel was excluded from evidence. `D11-F09 — RECONFIRMED / carried to remediation`.
- Activation/session TTL: before/at/after validity checks passed, but the actual activation TTL is 7 days from `CANDIDATE_POLICY_DEFAULTS_V1`, while N00 6.2A requires 24 hours. This is D12-F06 and was not changed here.
- Bot boundary: D12 did not execute Nam-owned Bot source/runtime/log retention. No Bot-private sentinel was copied into retention-generated audit data.

## Findings and ownership handoff

### D12-F03 — P1 — 90-day tournament-owned cleanup is not enforced

The policy predicate reaches `ELIGIBLE_FOR_REVIEW` at the inclusive 90-day cutoff, but the actual service only clears expired operational records. Terminal tournament, enrollment and ACK rows remain. Remediation requires Đức’s retention owner plus Long’s lifecycle/identity-mapping boundary. Do not delete shared `User` identity or other-tournament references.

### D12-F04 — P1 — 365-day audit retention is not configured or enforced

`AUDIT` is append-only but has `retentionMs: null`, and no cleanup/anonymization path exists in the current retention service. A verified 364/365/366-day PostgreSQL fixture retained all rows. Remediation requires the shared audit boundary and explicit identity-mapping/ACL policy; no cleanup was implemented in D12-B.

### D12-F05 — P2 — ACK evidence retention is unspecified

`ACK_EVIDENCE` is `MODULE_POLICY_REQUIRED` with no duration. Current behavior preserves ACK rows, but repository authority does not define whether they follow the 90-day rule or a separate compliance-evidence policy. Long must resolve the policy before implementation.

### D12-F06 — P2 — Activation token TTL is 7 days instead of approved 24 hours

The real activation service issued a token whose `expiresAt` was exactly 168 hours after issuance. N00 6.2A/acceptance D08 requires 24 hours. Before/at/after expiry behavior itself is correct and independent from retention; the candidate duration is not.

### D11-F09 — RECONFIRMED / carried to remediation

Token-like values in free-form audit `reason` remain unsanitized. D12-B verified that retention processing does not copy the synthetic unsafe reason into its own audit records. This remains the existing D11 finding and is not renumbered.
